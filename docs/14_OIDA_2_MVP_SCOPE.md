# OIDA 2.0 MVP Scope

## Hypothesis

AI can materially reduce preparation and reconciliation effort across one real
delivery loop while humans retain correction and final authority.

## In scope

- One project workspace, frame, authority assignments, and versioned sources.
- Grounded context analysis and requirement candidates.
- Human candidate editing/rejection/regeneration and requirement baseline commit.
- Grounded solution outline, risks, work plan, and QA/validation scope as one
  delivery-baseline candidate and gate.
- Idempotent materialization and basic execution/validation updates.
- Typed requirement-to-work-to-validation-to-evidence traceability.
- AI blocker, drift, coverage, consistency, and evidence-gap findings.
- Project Truth/readiness projection with explicit freshness and uncertainty.
- AI-prepared acceptance package and human-only final acceptance.
- Automation policy, audit, honest AI/integration failure, and reversible action.
- Tenant/project/service access isolation, explicit owner bindings, and fail-closed
  identity/entitlement enforcement.
- Idempotent owner-boundary materialization with read-after-write reconciliation;
  technical/test evidence remains distinct from customer acceptance evidence.

## Out of scope

Project memory, full automated change impact, cross-project portfolio, advanced
attention inbox, deep bidirectional integrations, production deployment, contract
or commercial commitment, infrastructure inventory, generic workflow designer,
graph/vector platforms as product features, multi-agent autonomy, predictive
analytics, and executive dashboard suites.

## MVP proof scenario

A delivery lead imports a small software-project brief, resolves AI-identified
ambiguities, commits requirements, adjusts and commits the generated delivery
baseline, executes a representative subset of work/tests, attaches evidence,
handles at least one failure and waiver/rework path, and an authorized sponsor
makes final acceptance from a grounded package.

## Exit measures

- The closed loop completes without off-system reconstruction of core truth.
- All committed AI-derived facts retain source/actor/version provenance.
- Zero unauthorized baseline, risk, waiver, or acceptance actions succeed.
- Unauthorized project/source data never enters Project Truth or an AI context;
  identity/entitlement failure produces an explicit blocked state.
- A rejected/regenerated candidate never corrupts a baseline.
- AI and integration failure paths remain usable and truthful.
- Baseline evaluation shows measurable preparation time saved and records human
  correction/acceptance rates; numeric targets are an open product decision.
